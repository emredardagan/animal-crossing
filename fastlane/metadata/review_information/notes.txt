Thank you for reviewing Paw Crossing.

How to play: swipe to steer, tap to hop forward.

Where things are:
- In-app purchases: tap the shop (storefront) button on the title screen. Products: Paw Club, Remove Ads, Safari Pack, Legendary Pack (non-consumable) and three coin packs (consumable). Restore Purchases is in Settings (gear button).
- Rewarded ads: after a run that scores 10 or more, the game-over panel offers "Continue" and "Coins x2". The shop has a "Free coins" rewarded ad and the daily gift has a "x2" rewarded ad.
- Interstitial ads appear only between runs, never during play, and not in the first session or the first three runs.
- The App Tracking Transparency prompt appears once, after the first run ends, behind our own short explainer.
- Consent (UMP) is shown at launch in regions where it is required.

No account or login is needed. Progress syncs with iCloud key-value storage.
