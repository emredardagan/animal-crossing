Hop a little animal across roads, rivers, railways, frozen lakes and stampedes. How far can you go?

Paw Crossing is a cozy 3D arcade hopper. Swipe to steer, tap to hop forward, and don't look back: the world keeps moving.

SIX SEASONS
Start in Sunny Meadow, then hop through Autumn Woods, Snowy Pass, Sandy Beach, Cactus Canyon and the neon streets of Neon City. Every season has its own hazards, its own weather and its own big set piece.

STAMPEDES AND STORMS
Dodge a cow stampede, a penguin parade, a crab rave and a safari rush. Night falls, storms roll in with lightning, blizzards hide the road. Slide across frozen lakes, ride logs and boats, and watch for lily pads that dive under.

24 PETS TO COLLECT
Start with a dog, a cat, a bunny or a chick, and unlock foxes, pandas, penguins, lions, a bee, a fish and more with the coins you grab on the way.

MISSIONS AND POWER-UPS
Three missions at a time keep every run fresh. Grab hearts for an extra life, magnets that pull in coins and gems worth three coins each. Dawdle too long and an eagle swoops in!

PAW CLUB
One purchase, yours forever: no ads between runs, double coins, a triple daily gift, the Golden Dog and a paw badge. No subscriptions.

Plays fully offline. Your progress syncs across your devices with iCloud.
