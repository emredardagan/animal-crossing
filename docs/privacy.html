Privacy Policy

Paw Crossing for iPhone · Last updated October 2, 2026

The short version

Paw Crossing has no accounts and we do not run any servers that collect your data. The game shows ads from Google AdMob and sells optional one-time purchases through Apple, with RevenueCat keeping track of them. Those partners receive the data described below.

Data stored on your device and in your iCloud

Your progress (best score, coins, pets, missions) is stored on your device and, if iCloud is on, in your own iCloud key-value storage so it follows you to your other devices. We cannot see it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when iOS asks), approximate location from your IP address, ad interactions, and crash and performance diagnostics. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first and can change your choice anytime in Settings › Privacy choices. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history so your purchases unlock on all your devices. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

Paw Crossing is not directed at children under 13, and ads are limited to content rated for general audiences (G).

Your choices

You can deny tracking in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, reset your advertising identifier, buy Remove Ads or Paw Club to stop ads between runs, or delete the app to remove its local data.
