Paw Crossing Support

Help for the iPhone game

How do I play?

Swipe left, right or back to steer and tap to hop forward. Keep moving: the camera keeps creeping forward, and an eagle comes for pets that wait too long.

My purchases are missing

Open Settings (the gear on the title screen) and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

My coins and pets on a new device

Turn on iCloud for Paw Crossing (iOS Settings › your name › iCloud). Progress syncs automatically between devices signed in to the same Apple ID.

The game feels slow

Settings › Graphics › Low turns off ambient occlusion and lowers the resolution.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/emredardagan/animal-crossing/issues and we'll get back to you.
